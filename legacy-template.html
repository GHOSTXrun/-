NOXVIL / CORE Make every trading decision traceable

Six roles · one workflow · one shared record

From trade ideas to reasons behind every action.

NOXVIL is a collaborative terminal for trading research and strategy operations. Clear decision stages bring signals, risk, positions, execution and equity into one inspectable workspace.

01 / SPECIALIZATION

Clear responsibilities

Discovery and execution have separate responsibilities. Scout proposes candidates, Guard checks conditions and Sizer defines exposure through explicit inputs and outputs.

02 / RISK BEFORE ACTION

Checks before action

Quote freshness, liquidity, position limits and costs belong to the same decision chain. Failed checks retain their reasons so uncertainty remains visible.

03 / ONE LEDGER

Results return to the ledger

One session drives positions, logs and the equity curve. Net P&L includes costs; trace IDs connect signals, actions and settlement.

Implemented

Roadmap

Operating boundaries

The main workspace uses synthetic market replay. The independent market workspace reports its connection status. No real wallet is connected and no on-chain orders are submitted.

All tools in one workspace

Start the terminal → inspect candidates and safety checks → observe OPEN / CLOSED → reconcile the ledger and equity after settlement. Controls, filters and exports remain available.

How the six roles collaborate

Scout discovers candidates; Guard can veto; Sizer allocates current equity; Trigger replays entries and exits; Scribe records reasons; Ledger reconciles balances. These are deterministic workflow stages, with no LLM integration.

Data, costs and persistence

Quotes are deterministic synthetic fixtures. Each side includes configured adverse slippage and a 0.1% fee. The ledger counts closed trades; available cash excludes open position costs. Saved sessions can resume after refresh. Closing the browser stops execution. Export regularly because browser storage can be cleared.

The overnight concept and production execution

The original $33.40 → $13,456.22 story, 71 trades, 47 wins, 24 losses and 23 filtered tokens is an unverified concept scenario, excluded from this ledger. This workspace has no live quotes, token safety service, wallet signing or exchange execution. Production trading requires a persistent backend, isolated signing and transaction reconciliation.
