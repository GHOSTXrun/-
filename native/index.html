SESSION / RISK POLICY

Creates a fresh paper ledger. Export first. No real funds or wallet are connected.

DATA / MODEL NOTES

Native implementation

This page is a native HTML/JavaScript application. Six deterministic roles share one persistent paper ledger. They are not LLM agents.

Real market observations

Solana watchlist and aggregate USD prices come from DEX Screener. Recent profiles are discovery metadata; they may include old or promoted tokens. Fetch time does not prove the upstream pool quote changed at that instant. A source link is provided for the selected pair.

Guard is limited

Checks price retrieval age, reported USD liquidity, hourly volume and recent-sell metadata. There is no on-chain sell simulation, honeypot scanner, mint/freeze-authority verification or secured signer. LIMITED is not a token safety approval.

Paper fill model

Allocation uses available paper cash. Fills use aggregate USD price plus a constant-product reserve impact approximation, not an executable DEX quote. Modeled fees are 0.1% on each side. Actual pool fees, routes, MEV and transaction confirmation are not modeled.

Paper strategy

When armed: positive 5m change + basic screen → one position. Net take profit, stop loss, maximum hold or session loss limit trigger a modeled exit only on a fresh held-pair observation. Pausing stops automatic paper orders; observations continue. Manual closes also require a fresh quote and estimated impact within policy. No quote means no new fill.

Storage and runtime

Cash, positions, trades and decisions are stored in this browser. Refresh restores the strategy paused. Closing the browser stops operation. Export JSON for a full audit and CSV for closed trades. The chart shows the latest 600 observed marks. This application contains no fabricated profit history and no synthetic quote fallback.
